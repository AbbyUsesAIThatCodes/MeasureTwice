# Measure Twice Reference

## Read From Zero

Use the zero graduation as your starting point. A graduation is a mark on the ruler; an interval is the space between neighboring marks. Count equal intervals and keep the measurement unit with the number.

## Fractions And Units

The denominator tells how many equal intervals make one whole unit. The numerator counts those intervals. Equivalent fractions name the same length. For a mixed number, include the whole units before counting the fractional part.

## Choose, Cut, And Inspect

Choose a visible ruler position with the pointer or arrow keys. Cut commits your measurement. Read the inspection after cutting, then choose Keep Piece or Try Again. A hint or worked lesson provides practice support.

## Explore The Workshop

Free Play lets you choose lengths and instruments. Learn offers Start at Zero, Meet the Fractions, Same Length, Different Names, Whole Inches and a Little More, and Will It Fit? Challenge offers independent House, Plane, Chair, and Tree builds. You can revisit lessons and replay builds.

Progress stays in this browser. A different device or site address has separate saved work. Automatic assembly is a reward for practice; it does not demonstrate physical tool use or independent measurement mastery.
