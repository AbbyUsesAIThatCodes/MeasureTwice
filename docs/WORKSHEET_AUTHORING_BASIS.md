## Startup And Controls

A fresh or empty-save visit opens **Free Play**, with a 3-Inch Ruler and no target. A valid active saved build resumes the Challenge chooser and retains held inspection, attempts, partial/completed models and replay badges. Progress is local to the same browser origin; opening a different port/device does not transfer it. Free Play/Learn retain their existing page-only sessions. There is no documented deep-link startup control.

Use Free Play, Learn or Challenge at the top. Choose Instrument explicitly starts a new Free Play measurement workspace and clears comparison pieces while retaining response records. Presets: 3-, 6- and 12-Inch Rulers, 36-Inch Yardstick, 15- and 30-Centimetre Rulers, and Metre Stick (100 cm). Changing instruments does not convert an unfinished cut.

Click or drag on the ruler, drag the blue workshop cut pointer, or focus the ruler and use Left/Right arrows. All use the same visible intervals. Home selects zero; zero cannot be cut. End selects the instrument endpoint. Enter or Cut commits one attempt. Show Zero scrolls the ruler back to its origin; Magnify Ruler enlarges it. Hidden incompatible scale/target choices are explained beside the selector. Learn permits exact compatible scales; Challenge fixes the scale for the current recipe step.

Correctness appears after Cut. Inspect Needed and Your Piece: a short piece has a red missing extension, a long piece has red excess, and a correct one is green. Challenge/Learn use Try Again for an incorrect cut or Keep Piece for a correct cut. Free Play can keep a mismatch as a comparison. Kept model pieces duplicate and assemble automatically; the table stays visible. Reduced Motion or Skip Animation reaches the same held inspection; acknowledgement is still required. Mute controls sound.

Choose A Lesson opens any of five Learn entries. Show Me demonstrates; Try With Less Help switches to practice. Correct cuts plus any authored lesson question advance the ten guided/practice stages. Earlier demonstration, Learn/reference exposure, hints and retries are assistance, not fresh independent evidence.

Choose A Build opens House, Plane, Chair and Tree independently. Question-mark tiles reveal exact-recipe miniatures after completion. Build Again appends a run while retaining the badge and original records. Build Progress offers a text Build Report and JSON Export Progress. Supporting Questions are the original thirty-item reference bank, not an obligatory quiz or completion score. Downloads must be submitted by the student/teacher; the game does not submit to Classroom.

## Learn Teacher Key

| Lesson | Guided Target | Practice Target | Authored Choice Answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start At Zero | 3/8 in | 5/8 in | The Zero Graduation |
| Meet The Fractions | 5/8 in | 7/16 in | No separate choice question |
| Same Length, Different Names | 3/4 in | 1/4 in | Yes, the Same Endpoint |
| Whole Inches And A Little More | 1 3/16 in | 1 5/8 in | No separate choice question |
| Will It Fit? | 1 1/4 in | 1 1/4 in | It Is Shorter Than the Opening |

Start At Zero: the **graduation marks/graduations are the lines**, and **intervals are the spaces**. Three 1/8-inch intervals = 3/8 inch. On sixteenths, six 1/16-inch intervals = 6/16 = 3/8 inch; each pair groups into 1/8 inch. Coarser halves/quarters cannot show that exact target and are hidden.

Same Length, Different Names practice uses 2/8 = 1/4, with Yes, the Same Endpoint as its answer. The guided example uses 3/4 = 6/8. Whole Inches And A Little More: 1 3/16 = 19/16; omitting the whole inch loses 1 inch. Will It Fit: 1 1/8 versus needed 1 1/4 leaves a 1/8-inch gap; 1 3/8 versus needed 1 1/4 has 1/8 inch excess.
