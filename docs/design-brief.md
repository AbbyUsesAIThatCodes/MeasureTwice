# MeasureTwice Design Brief

The teacher's September 27 woodshop concept became the approved [Game Design](game-design.md) on September 28, 2026. Read that document for current behavior. This brief retains the founding purpose and the decisions that developed from it.

Students turn numerical and fractional inch measurements into ruler positions, cut virtual wood, and watch accepted pieces duplicate and assemble into recognizable models. Design remains grounded in [Design And Modeling 1.3 Measuring Matters](https://github.com/AbbyUsesAIThatCodes/DesignAndModeling26-27/tree/main/units/01-introduction-to-design/1.3-measuring-matters), with sources and limits in [Curriculum Alignment](curriculum-alignment.md).

## Three Connected Mechanics

| Mechanic | Student Action and Visible Result | Learning Connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predict the Length | Select a position; a neutral mark appears and the plank moves relative to the saw. | Equal intervals, fractional inches, equivalence, mixed numbers, origin, and units. |
| Cut and Inspect | Commit with Cut; every valid choice gets sawn, then swings into a closeup comparing selected and required lengths. | A measurement has a visible consequence. Incorrect attempts show excess or missing material after commitment. |
| Keep Duplicate and Assemble | A correct piece moves right, visibly supplies all required copies, then flies into the model. A wrong piece moves left after inspection. | Measured parts have consistent lengths. Assembly is motivating context, not proof of physical construction skill. |

## Preserved Requirements

- Free Play, Learn, and Challenge share the same measurement geometry and tools.
- One successful measurement supplies each distinct target length per model; retries are allowed and duplicate parts do not require duplicate correct answers.
- Keep equivalent fractions grouped by physical length and count the original among required copies.
- Begin with a model house, then consider airplane, tree, and other familiar classroom Dash subjects.
- Use a bright, cartoony 3D workshop, readable ruler, compact overlays, and laptop controls.
- Keep curriculum links and every Learn/Challenge entry documented.

## Superseded Proposal

The [September 27 brief](https://github.com/AbbyUsesAIThatCodes/MeasureTwice/blob/28b5ff1a95ecfda72f6039fcd43a1cf89d70bc7b/docs/design-brief.md) gated cutting on a correct response. The teacher's September 28 decision replaces that behavior: cut every valid committed selection and reveal correctness during post-cut inspection. Earlier previews are exploratory history, not implementation requirements.

The [Approved Workshop Mockup](mockups/predict-cut-inspect/README.md), archived in merged PR #10, establishes the interface composition and visual/interaction direction. Preserve that accepted reference; its documented prototype shortcuts do not change the game contract.

See [Game Modes](game-modes.md), [Curriculum Content](curriculum-content.md), [Ruler Interaction](ruler-interaction.md), and [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for the current planning foundation. Production engine, exact house geometry/cut schedule, access behavior, explanation-response UI, and tuned animation timings remain to be resolved within the approved direction.
