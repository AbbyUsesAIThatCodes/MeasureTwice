## What Changed And What Was Preserved

- C01–C06 retain public IDs, goals and answers. C04 uses the prior chair-rail context. C05 retains the public fit judgment and the prior draft’s separate 1 1/4-inch cut, recorded before placement. No concept response is inferred from a later correct cut.
- C07–C18 retain all prior fractional/mixed targets. C19–C22 preserve unreduced prompts and both unequal examples: 6/16 is not 3/4; 14/8 is not 1 1/2. C23–C26 retain short/long/exact/short comparisons. C27–C28 retain origin choices. C29–C30 retain original-plus-copies accounting; copy planning remains a local extension.
- Current interaction-only direction supersedes required typed explanation fields for C03, C05 and C19–C26, and the Learn reasoning prompts. Correct choices and measured endpoints can complete current automatic components; they do not demonstrate independent explanation. No written response is automatically passed, deleted, or rewritten. Existing `:explanation` records remain exported for teacher review. This is a reduction in collected evidence, explicitly reported rather than called equivalent assessment.
- Original authored prompts, answers, goal mappings, feedback, explanation flags and public/prior differences are preserved in [the complete reconciliation record](CHALLENGE_RECONCILIATION.json). Existing historical exports remain unchanged.
- Fabrication order stays chair → plane → house. Difficulty is deliberately nonmonotonic (the first measured chair leg uses 1 1/16 in before later halves/quarters); prerequisite fractional-inch Learn work is freely accessible. This preserves prior construction design, not a claim of a newly scaffolded curriculum sequence. EasyAsPie precedes equivalent-fraction work in the teacher’s sequence; no new compulsory conversion lesson is added.
